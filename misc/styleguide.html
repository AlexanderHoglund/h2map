Thaduberg

Style guide

The design language of the Thaduberg platform, extracted from the running code. The voice is technical-modern: simple minimal boxes on a bright surface, straight lines, no ornament — a precise instrument rather than a marketing page. Everything here mirrors apps/web/app/globals.css and apps/web/components/ui/, which remain the source of truth.

1. Principles

  • Light only. There is no dark mode. color-scheme: light is pinned so native controls, scrollbars and <select> popups stay light even when the OS prefers dark. Never add a dark: variant.
  • Square. The entire Tailwind radius scale is zeroed, so every rounded-* utility renders square. Only rounded-full survives (switch knobs, dots, spinners).
  • Hairlines, not shadows. Structure comes from 1px neutral-300 borders on white. Shadows appear only on things that float above the page (dropdowns, modals).
  • The number is the hero. Chrome recedes; figures are tabular, aligned and never dimmed below AA.
  • Say where a number came from. Every value carries its provenance (override / derived / benchmark) — see §8.

5. Shape & spacing

  • Radius: 0. Boxes are square, everywhere.
  • Borders: 1px neutral-300. Dashed neutral-300 marks a collapsed “advanced” fold or an empty state.
  • Card padding: p-3 (12px) in dense workspace panels, p-4–p-6 on standalone pages.
  • Field grid: two columns from sm up (grid gap-3 sm:grid-cols-2); full-width fields span both.
  • Rhythm: gaps of 2 / 3 / 4 (8/12/16px). Sections separate by gap-4 in the results grid.

6. Buttons

components/ui/Button.tsx — variant: primary | secondary | ghost, size: sm | md.

  • One primary per view. Everything else is secondary or ghost.
  • sm is the workspace default (toolbars, table rows); md for page-level actions and forms.
  • Destructive actions are secondary buttons with text-red-700 plus an inline confirm — never a lone red button that fires immediately.

7. Forms & fields

  • Label above, 12px medium neutral-600; unit suffix inside the control on the right.
  • Focus is border-brand + a 2px brand/40 ring — never outline: none alone.
  • Disabled greys the input surface only. Never opacity-dim the whole block: dimmed labels and notes fail contrast, and the note is information (“forced to 0 under this sourcing”), not chrome.

8. Provenance badges

The signature pattern of this app: every resolved value says where it came from, and an overridden value keeps its benchmark one keystroke away.

Fuel consumption override

benchmark: 2,638 t/vessel/yr · restore

override derived benchmark unverified
  • override — your typed value (renders in brand blue).
  • derived — computed from other inputs (purple).
  • benchmark — reference data (neutral).
  • unverified — a benchmark flagged as planning-level.

Other badge tones: neutral brand warning — components/ui/Badge.tsx.

9. Notices & callouts

Saved — a transient flash, cleared after ~3s.

Legacy accounting loaded: this scenario charges the merchant fuel price AND production CAPEX/O&M — production cost is counted twice.

Sign-in failed — check your email and password.

  • Amber = a model caveat the reader must know (never silent).
  • Green = a completed action, transient.
  • Red = a failure the user can act on. Say what to do, not just what broke.

11. Workspace tabs

The corridor workspace has one nav bar. Each tab with a work domain carries that domain's colour from the MMMCZCS process model (the --domain-* tokens): Vessels light blue, Fuel = Energy green, Port orange, Regulation & Financing magenta. Intro and Results have no domain and stay neutral on the brand tone; Projects sits outside the walk in grey.   • The delta pair is a CVD-safe blue↔red diverging pair; colour is never the only cue — a legend swatch and an axis label always accompany it.
  • Separate the outlier, never compress the rest. If one value exceeds the series median by more than 5×, the default rendering is wrong: decompose by cost nature, split the series, offset the axis (and label the break) — or drop the series and state its shape in words. warnIfDominated() enforces this in development, per series.
  • No log axis for money in a non-technical panel; no broken axis on a primary series.
  • Fixed colour domains on the map — a colour means the same LCOH everywhere on that layer, never rescaled to the viewport. The LCOH ramp matches the Chilean reference tool: 0–10 USD/kg, eight stops (0.0 · 1.8 · 3.0 · 4.5 · 5.5 · 6.8 · 8.0 · ≥10.0), red = cheap → green → blue = expensive, with each end pinned to its own reserved stop so out-of-range values stay distinct. This is a deliberate, requested departure from the CVD-safe ramp guidance — the numeric ticks remain the non-colour encoding.

13. Accessibility rules

Every page is checked with axe (serious + critical must be zero) in the e2e suite. Rules that have actually bitten, kept here so they don't again:

Do

  • Darken muted text to clear AA on the page, not just white.
  • Grey a disabled input's surface; keep its label and note readable.
  • Pair every colour cue with a swatch, label or badge.
  • Give icon-only controls an aria-label; modals a role="dialog" + accessible name.
  • Keep <dt>/<dd> as direct children of a <dl>'s wrapper div.

Don't

  • Use opacity to show disabled state on a whole block.
  • Use amber-700 on an amber wash (fails AA) — use 800.
  • Use neutral-500 on a tinted tab or wash.
  • Rely on red/green alone to carry meaning.
  • Animate without honouring prefers-reduced-motion.

14. Writing

  • Plain and specific. “This site runs 32% of the year; the carrier's synthesis loop needs 85%” — not “suboptimal duty cycle”.
  • Name the consequence. Deleting says what is lost; expiry says scenarios are kept.
  • State uncertainty. A screening estimate shows its range and names the driver of the spread. Never render precision the number doesn't have.
  • Never silent. Loading under a newer engine, a legacy accounting quirk, an unverified benchmark — all announced inline.
  • Sentence case for labels and buttons; units always stated; en dashes in ranges ($2,209–$3,027).
